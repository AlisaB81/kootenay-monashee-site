Need help finding where to vote?

Not sure where to vote? Fill out the form below and our local EDA team can help you find your voting information.

This service is provided by the Kootenay–Monashee OneBC Electoral District Association (EDA). This website is not operated by Elections BC.

Canadian numbers such as 250-555-0123 or +1 (250) 555-0123.

For example, V1A 1A1 or V1A1A1.

Enter an email address, a phone number, or both so our team can contact you.

Your information will be sent to the Kootenay–Monashee OneBC EDA team through Formspree. The team will use your address to look up voting information and contact you using the details you provide. This form does not return an automatic voting-location result.
