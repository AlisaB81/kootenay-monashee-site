Privacy notice (draft for review)

This is a draft, not a finalized privacy notice. The EDA should review and complete the outstanding details below before treating it as final.

Voting-help requests

The Kootenay–Monashee OneBC Electoral District Association (EDA) provides the voting-help service. If you submit a request after the form is configured, it will collect your first and last name, street address, city, postal code, and the email address and/or phone number you choose to provide. The EDA will use this information to respond to your request by looking up voting information using the address you provide and contacting you with a response. This is not an instant automated lookup. The form sends submissions to Formspree for processing and delivery according to the EDA's Formspree configuration.

A request for voting help is not treated as consent to receive campaign news or as an indication of political support. The optional campaign-updates checkbox is separate and unchecked by default. Its choice and the consent wording/version are submitted separately.

Details to confirm before publication

  • Confirm the Formspree account's configured recipient(s) and who can access submissions.
  • Confirm where Formspree processes or stores submitted information.
  • State the applicable retention and deletion practices.
  • Confirm the endpoint's spam-protection settings, including the honeypot field.
  • Provide the EDA's designated privacy contact and process for questions.

These details are intentionally left open for EDA review rather than assuming recipient settings, access, storage arrangements, privacy contacts, or retention periods.
