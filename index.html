Welcome to the Kootenay-Monashee Electoral District Association

Mission Statement

The OneBC Monashee Electoral District is a grass roots movement of the people to restore strong leadership in our province's political sphere and to live up to our potential as an economic leader while maintaining high environmental standards. We want to get back to why people live here; to prosper, to enjoy, and to belong.

Purpose

Our Mission is to restore the Kootenay's economic and social prosperity. Change needs to happen and we want healthy communities to thrive again in our region.

Values

The Kootenay-Monashee EDA is committed to the guiding principles of OneBC's approach to managing our economics, health care, and communities. We value the livelihoods of our community members and want to increase the quality of life and living standards for the people of this province.

Goals

Our goals are to remove the red tape and promote economic development, responsibly manage tax payer money, give hope and provide resources to the vulnerable in our communities, provide timely and exceptional health care, and ensure personal rights to property and land.

Meet Our OneBC Candidate

Stephen Hill

Stephen Hill - Candidate for Kootenay-Monashee

View Stephen on OneBC

Stephen Hill is your ONEBC candidate for Kootenay–Monashee. For 32 years, Stephen and his wife have called this community home, raising their family and building a successful business. Their life in the Kootenays has been shaped by strong local connections and a shared commitment to giving back.

Community involvement has always been an important part of Stephen’s life. He founded the LeRoi Community Foundation in Trail and was one of the founding members of the Trail Youth Justice Committee. Over the years, he has also served on numerous boards, including Red Mountain Racers, contributing his time and experience to organizations that bring people together and support the community.

Now, Stephen is bringing that experience and commitment to his candidacy. As a longtime resident, business owner, and community volunteer, he understands the dedication it takes to build a life here. He looks forward to connecting with residents across Kootenay–Monashee, listening to their concerns, and working toward a strong future for the region he and his family call home.
