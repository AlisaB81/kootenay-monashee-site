Welcome to the Kootenay-Monashee Electoral District Association

Mission Statement

The ONEBC Kootenay–Monashee Electoral District Association is a grassroots movement working to restore accountable leadership, defend individual freedoms, and create opportunity. We believe British Columbia should be a place where hard work pays off, businesses can grow, and families can build a secure future. Here in the Kootenays, that means supporting local livelihoods, caring for our natural environment, and strengthening the communities we call home.

Purpose

Our mission is to restore prosperity and confidence in Kootenay–Monashee. We want government to respect taxpayers, remove barriers to opportunity, and deliver the essential services people depend on. By bringing local voices forward, we aim to help build a province where people can afford to live, work, raise a family, and retire.

Values

We stand for individual freedom, private property rights, personal responsibility, and accountable government. We value strong families, safe communities, thriving local businesses, and responsible stewardship of our land and resources. We believe public institutions should serve people effectively and elected representatives should listen to the communities they represent.

Goals

We are working toward lower taxes, fewer unnecessary regulations, and an economy that supports small businesses and our region’s resource industries. Our priorities include responsible public spending, timely access to quality healthcare, safer communities, and meaningful treatment and recovery for people struggling with addiction. We will champion property rights and practical policies that help people build a better life in Kootenay–Monashee.

Meet Our OneBC Candidate

Stephen Hill

Stephen Hill - Candidate for Kootenay-Monashee

View Stephen on OneBC

Stephen Hill is your ONEBC candidate for Kootenay–Monashee. For 32 years, Stephen and his wife have called this community home, raising their family and building a successful business. Their life in the Kootenays has been shaped by strong local connections and a shared commitment to giving back.

Community involvement has always been an important part of Stephen’s life. He founded the LeRoi Community Foundation in Trail and was one of the founding members of the Trail Youth Justice Committee. Over the years, he has also served on numerous boards, including Red Mountain Racers, contributing his time and experience to organizations that bring people together and support the community.

Now, Stephen is bringing that experience and commitment to his candidacy. As a longtime resident, business owner, and community volunteer, he understands the dedication it takes to build a life here. He looks forward to connecting with residents across Kootenay–Monashee, listening to their concerns, and working toward a strong future for the region he and his family call home.
