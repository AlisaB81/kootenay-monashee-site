Meet Our OneBC Candidate

Stephen Hill

Stephen Hill - Candidate for Kootenay-Monashee

View Stephen on OneBC

Stephen Hill is your ONEBC candidate for Kootenay–Monashee. For 32 years, Stephen and his wife have called this community home, raising their family and building a successful business. Their life in the Kootenays has been shaped by strong local connections and a shared commitment to giving back.

Community involvement has always been an important part of Stephen’s life. He founded the LeRoi Community Foundation in Trail and was one of the founding members of the Trail Youth Justice Committee. Over the years, he has also served on numerous boards, including Red Mountain Racers, contributing his time and experience to organizations that bring people together and support the community.

Now, Stephen is bringing that experience and commitment to his candidacy. As a longtime resident, business owner, and community volunteer, he understands the dedication it takes to build a life here. He looks forward to connecting with residents across Kootenay–Monashee, listening to their concerns, and working toward a strong future for the region he and his family call home.

OneBC Platform

Affordability and Taxes

OneBC proposes reducing provincial personal and corporate income-tax rates and lowering the provincial sales tax to help households and businesses retain more of their income.

  • Reduce provincial income-tax rates.
  • Lower the PST.
  • Review regulatory and tax costs that contribute to housing costs.

Economy and Employment

OneBC’s economic approach emphasizes private investment, business growth and employment.

  • Review provincial regulations and reduce unnecessary administrative burdens.
  • Improve the predictability and timeliness of approvals.
  • Create conditions that encourage employers to invest and hire.
  • Pursue pipeline access to overseas markets through Prince Rupert or Kitimat.

Healthcare

OneBC supports private healthcare options alongside a fully funded public healthcare system.

  • Redirect resources from administration toward frontline care.
  • Support additional care options alongside public services.
  • Repeal the Health Professions and Occupations Act.

Education and Parental Rights

OneBC proposes a stronger focus on reading, writing and mathematics, alongside greater parental involvement and classroom transparency.

  • Strengthen standardized assessment of foundational skills.
  • Introduce a Parental Bill of Rights addressing informed consent and access to classroom information.
  • Remove SOGI 1 2 3 resources and content the party describes as politicized, while treating students and families respectfully.

Housing

OneBC proposes changes to provincial housing rules intended to make development more feasible and homeownership more attainable.

  • Eliminate the Energy Step Code.
  • Oppose municipal rent-control initiatives.
  • Open additional land for housing development.
  • Review taxes and regulatory costs that contribute to housing prices.

Responsible Government and Debt

OneBC’s published fiscal priorities include balancing the provincial budget within four years and directing part of future surpluses toward debt repayment.

  • Work toward a balanced budget within four years.
  • Allocate part of future surpluses to reducing debt.
  • Review spending and administration.

Immigration

OneBC calls for ending what its published materials describe as “mass immigration into B.C.” Immigration admissions and immigration status are federal responsibilities.

DRIPA, Property Rights and Accountability

OneBC proposes repealing British Columbia’s Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act, commonly called DRIPA. Repealing provincial legislation would not remove existing constitutional Indigenous rights.

  • Halt voluntary transfers of public land, resources and governmental control.
  • Seek greater accountability for public funding provided to Indigenous governments.
  • Advocate federal Indian Act reforms.
  • End mandatory government land acknowledgements.

Changes to provincial law and policy are distinct from Indian Act reforms, which require federal action.

Forestry and Natural Resources

OneBC proposes changes to support forestry operations and resource-sector investment.

  • Increase the allowable annual cut.
  • Reduce stumpage fees.
  • Expedite permit approvals and consultation processes.
  • Pursue pipeline access to tidewater.
  • Strengthen cooperation with western provinces on energy and shared provincial interests.

Public Safety, Drugs and Addiction

OneBC proposes shifting addiction policy toward treatment and recovery while prioritizing action against repeat violent offenders.

  • Replace supervised-consumption sites with recovery centres.
  • Halt new supervised-consumption sites.
  • End prescribed safer supply.
  • Provide involuntary treatment and housing in severe cases.
  • Prioritize repeat violent offenders and port security.
